Privacy policy

Easymax Express LLC operates this store. We collect what we need to fulfill your order: your name, email, shipping address and order details. Payments are processed by Stripe; we do not see or store your card number.

We share order details with our supplier and carriers so your order can be delivered. We use advertising and analytics tools from Meta and TikTok on this site, which may set cookies and record visits and purchases. We also use Microsoft Clarity, which records how visitors use this site (clicks, scrolling and page activity, through heatmaps and session replays) so we can improve it; Microsoft may use cookies for this and processes the data under the Microsoft Privacy Statement (https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement). We do not record what you type into payment forms, which are hosted by Stripe. You can block these tools with your browser settings.

We do not sell your personal information. To ask about, correct or delete your data, email info@eme-usa.shop.
