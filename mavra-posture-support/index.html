Questions, answered

Will it fix my posture?

No product can promise that. Posture Support is a physical reminder: it pulls gently when you slouch so you can correct yourself. It is not a medical device and does not treat any condition.

I have back or shoulder pain. Can I use it?

Please talk to your doctor or physical therapist first. We make no claims about pain relief.

How long should I wear it?

Start with 20–30 minutes a day and increase gradually. It is not meant to be worn while sleeping.

Can I wear it under clothes?

Yes, under a loose top or sweater. Under a fitted top the straps may show.

How does the sizing run?

Small. Use the weight chart and size up if in doubt. If it doesn't fit, we exchange it free within 30 days.
