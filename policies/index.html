Shipping and returns

Shipping

Orders within the United States ship free. Most orders ship in 3 to 5 business days and arrive within about a week. You get a tracking number by email when your order leaves. Products are fulfilled by our supplier's US warehouse. If an order runs late, we email you a new date and you can cancel for a full refund.

Returns

You can return an item within 60 days of delivery. Email info@eme-usa.shop with your order number and we send you a return authorization and the return address. Return shipping is paid by the customer. We refund the product price once the item arrives.

Damaged, defective or wrong items

If an item arrives damaged, defective or wrong, email us within 60 days. We replace it or refund you in full, and you keep it.

Refunds

Refunds go back to your original payment method through Stripe. Your bank may take several days to show them.
