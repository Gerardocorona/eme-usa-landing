Questions, answered

How does the sizing run?

Use the size guide above with your waist measurement. If it doesn't fit, we exchange it free within 30 days.

Will it roll down?

The high waistband is designed to stay in place during normal daily wear. Fit matters: a size too small is the most common reason shapewear rolls.

Can I wear it after having a baby or a C-section?

Many women look for gentle support after pregnancy. Every recovery is different, so please check with your doctor before wearing any compression garment postpartum or after surgery. Sculpt Brief is not a medical device.
