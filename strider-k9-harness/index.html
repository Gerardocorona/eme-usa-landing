For the dog who walks you

A collar puts the pull on the neck. A back-clip harness can make pulling easier. A front ring redirects that forward drive sideways, which gives you more control with less strain on both of you.

Control from the front

Clip the leash to the chest ring for training walks, or to the back ring once your dog walks loose.

Questions, answered

Will it stop my dog from pulling?

A front-clip harness gives you more control and discourages pulling, but it is a training aid, not a guarantee. It works best alongside consistent leash training.

Is this a service dog harness?

No. It is a walking and training harness. The hook-and-loop panel is for your own name patch. We do not sell it as service-animal identification, and misrepresenting a pet as a service animal is illegal in many states.

What size does my dog need?

Go by chest measurement first, then weight. Between sizes, choose the larger one and tighten the straps.

Is it machine washable?

Yes: gentle cycle, cold water, air dry. Close the buckles first.

What is it made of?

Polyester shell with a breathable mesh lining and a metal leash ring.
